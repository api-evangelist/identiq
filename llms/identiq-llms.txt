# Identiq

> Identiq operates a privacy-preserving, peer-to-peer identity validation network that lets member companies verify that a customer is known and trusted by other members without any party sharing or exposing personal data. Its patented anonymized protocol keeps identity data encrypted inside each member's own environment while answering trust queries across the network. Products span customer identification, sign-up validation, risk-based onboarding, payment optimization, and trust & safety across travel, e-commerce, ticketing, fintech, telco, and marketplaces.
